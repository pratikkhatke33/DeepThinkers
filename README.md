## 📌 What Is SafeTap?

SafeTap is a **24-hour hackathon MVP** designed to protect senior citizens from one of India's fastest-growing threats — **cyber scams disguised as government action**, commonly known as *"Digital Arrest"* fraud.

Scammers impersonate CBI, CID, or Cyber Crime officers, threatening seniors with fake arrest warrants, money laundering charges, and "digital detention" — all to coerce them into transferring large sums of money.

**SafeTap stops them in real time.**
